# Intelligence Page

The Intelligence page controls which AI models Plexo uses for conversations, reasoning, tasks, and memory.

## Two Sections

**Thinking & Conversation** — These models handle your conversations with Plexo and any tasks you assign. When you chat, ask questions, or create work, these models do the thinking.

**Memory & Understanding** — These models help Plexo remember your conversations and search through past interactions. They run in the background and are essential for Plexo to learn from your history.

## How Priority Works

Providers are listed in priority order. Plexo tries the top one first. If that provider is unavailable, slow, or over your budget, Plexo automatically moves to the next one in the list. You can reorder providers using the arrow buttons.

## Built-in AI

Every workspace includes Plexo's built-in AI at no cost. It appears at the bottom of both sections as a fallback — if all your other providers are unavailable, the built-in AI keeps things working.

## Adding Providers

Click "Add provider" at the bottom of either section. You can:
- **Use a paid AI service** — Connect Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or others with an API key.
- **Use your own server** — Point to a local Ollama installation for maximum privacy.

## Managing Models

If you connect your own AI server (like Ollama), you can browse and install models directly from the Intelligence page. Click on the server in your provider list, then click "Browse available models."
